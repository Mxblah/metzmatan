Choose one background for your character, which grants +20% to six chosen skills and a starting wealth formula. You may instead choose to create your own background by choosing six skills to gain additional training in, and taking 10+2d10 (or less) for your starting Wealth. The only restrictions are that a maximum of only one Weapons skill may be chosen, and neither Magic skill may be trained from a background.
