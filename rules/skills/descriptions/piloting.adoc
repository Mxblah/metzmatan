==== Piloting

Always includes a specialty in a specific class of vehicle. Knowledge of and ability to control vehicles, including planes, trains, and automobiles.

In general, Piloting is a trained-only skill. Characters with no ranks in Piloting for a specific class of vehicle usually cannot perform more than the bare minimum of basic or emergency maneuvers with that vehicle.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Identify an ordinary vehicle, turn on and off

|Simple (+50)
|Perform typical operations with a specific vehicle

|Ordinary (+25)
|Identify a specific type or model of vehicle, perform untrained emergency operations

|Straightforward (+10)
|Perform complex or unusual operations

|Normal (0)
|Typical evasive or in-combat maneuvering, determine capabilities of unknown vehicle

|Hard (-10)
|Push vehicle to its limits, determine capabilities of exotic vehicle

|Expert (-25)
|Perform typical operations with vehicle outside of specialty

|Extreme (-50)
|Perform complex operations with vehicle outside of specialty, perform typical operations with exotic vehicle outside of specialty

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Perform operations with magical vehicles outside of specialty

|===
