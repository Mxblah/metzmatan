==== Survival

Knowledge of the wilderness and ability to maintain oneself outside of traditional civilization.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Identify common plants or animals

|Simple (+50)
|Survive in gentle forest or around typical lake

|Ordinary (+25)
|Identify specific plants or animals, survive in typical forest

|Straightforward (+10)
|Survive in grasslands or hills

|Normal (0)
|Identify unusual or obscure plants or animals

|Hard (-10)
|Survive in mountains or tundra

|Expert (-25)
|Survive in desert, identify exotic or mythical plants or animals

|Extreme (-50)
|Survive in wastelands, determine properties of unknown or magical plants or animals

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Survive in actively harmful terrain

|===
