==== Swim

Ability to float in and cross bodies of water or other liquids.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Swimming pool shallow enough to stand in

|Simple (+50)
|Typical swimming pool

|Ordinary (+25)
|Calm lake or similar still water

|Straightforward (+10)
|Typical river or similar flowing water

|Normal (0)
|Fast river, choppy lake

|Hard (-10)
|River rapids, stormy lake, deep ocean

|Expert (-25)
|Powerful or tricky rapids, stormy ocean

|Extreme (-50)
|Whirlpool, hurricane

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Up a strong waterfall, devastating storm

|===
