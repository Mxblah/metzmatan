==== Mechanics

Knowledge of and ability to repair and make use of mechanical devices, including many machines.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Use or maintain a simple mechanical device

|Simple (+50)
|Perform maintenance or repairs on typical machines or vehicles

|Ordinary (+25)
|Operate or maintain nonstandard machines

|Straightforward (+10)
|Determine functionality of standard but unknown devices

|Normal (0)
|Override or modify simple devices with appropriate tools

|Hard (-10)
|Decipher or repair complex devices

|Expert (-25)
|Override or modify complex devices

|Extreme (-50)
|Decipher magical devices

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Override or modify magical electronic devices

|===
