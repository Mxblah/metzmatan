==== Navigation

Ability to maintain direction and avoid getting lost.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Comfortably navigate a typical city with a phone or GPS, tell cardinal directions

|Simple (+50)
|Navigate with a map, correlate position with a map

|Ordinary (+25)
|Navigate unaided in a typical forest or city

|Straightforward (+10)
|Navigate with a map through a relatively featureless or complex location

|Normal (0)
|Explore a maze or simple labyrinth

|Hard (-10)
|Explore a labyrinth or complex maze

|Expert (-25)
|Navigate unaided through a featureless or complex location

|Extreme (-50)
|Navigate empty space or a constantly mutating location

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Explore a shifting or dreamscape labyrinth

|===
