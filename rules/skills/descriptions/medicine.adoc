==== Medicine

Knowledge of healthcare and ability to treat injuries. Useful in healing others or oneself.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Use typical first-aid supplies for minor injuries

|Simple (+50)
|Apply injections or other simple medical procedures, diagnose common injuries or illnesses

|Ordinary (+25)
|Diagnose typical injuries or illnesses

|Straightforward (+10)
|Stabilize an injured person, treat common injuries or illnesses

|Normal (0)
|Diagnose unusual injuries or illnesses, stabilize a dying person

|Hard (-10)
|Perform complex medical procedures

|Expert (-25)
|Perform major surgery, diagnose unusual magical injuries or illnesses

|Extreme (-50)
|Perform simple magical procedures or complex healing rites

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Remove curses, get insurance to pay for something on the first try

|===
