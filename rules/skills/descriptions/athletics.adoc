==== Acrobatics

Ability to move one's Body with power and endurance. Sprinting, jumping, and combat maneuvers. Secondary physical skill.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Climb a ladder, spend the day walking

|Simple (+50)
|Climb a tree or latticed/knotted rope, run a mile

|Ordinary (+25)
|Climb a typical rope, tear fabric

|Straightforward (+10)
|Climb an uneven wall, break sturdy glass

|Normal (0)
|Force open an ordinary door

|Hard (-10)
|Climb a mountain or sheer surface with some handholds, force open a sturdy door

|Expert (-25)
|Climb a ceiling, break chains or small metal objects

|Extreme (-50)
|Scale a smooth surface, force open a sturdy metal door

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Puncture sturdy metal plates, force open a security door

|===
