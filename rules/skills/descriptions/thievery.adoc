==== Thievery

Ability to manipulate objects with fine precision. Includes lockpicking, stealing, and disarming delicate mechanisms.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Steal an item from an unattended container

|Simple (+50)
|Pick or bypass a shoddy lock

|Ordinary (+25)
|Pick a simple lock

|Straightforward (+10)
|Avoid triggering a simple trap

|Normal (0)
|Pick a typical lock, disable a simple trap, avoid triggering a typical trap

|Hard (-10)
|Pick a good quality lock, disable a typical trap

|Expert (-25)
|Pick a complex lock, avoid triggering or disable a complex trap

|Extreme (-50)
|Pick a masterwork or magical lock, avoid triggering or disable a masterwork or magical trap

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Sabotage or rework masterwork or magical traps

|===
