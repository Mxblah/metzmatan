==== Tracking

Ability to locate and follow others.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Follow fresh tracks in mud or similar surfaces

|Simple (+50)
|Detect path of a large group on a path or trail

|Ordinary (+25)
|Detect tracks of large animals in a forest or similar

|Straightforward (+10)
|Tail an unaware target through a city or similar

|Normal (0)
|Follow tracks of individual people, tail a moderately cautious target

|Hard (-10)
|Detect tracks of small animals, detect tracks of larger animals or people after rain

|Expert (-25)
|Detect tracks of tiny animals, tail an aware target

|Extreme (-50)
|Detect tracks on bare rock or similar surfaces

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Detect old tracks through a shifting desert, follow magical signatures

|===
