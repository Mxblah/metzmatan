==== Occult

Affinity for esoteric magic and the supernatural generally. Includes knowledge and abilities that complement lacre or ichor. Secondary magical skill, often associated with Celestial Magic.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Assist in a rite as a secondary participant

|Simple (+50)
|Perform a simple rite as the primary caster

|Ordinary (+25)
|Resist mental strain from typical artifacts

|Straightforward (+10)
|Recall basic or broad supernatural knowledge

|Normal (0)
|Decipher esoteric writing, determine esoteric language

|Hard (-10)
|Comprehend a typical unknown rite or spell, recall specific supernatural knowledge

|Expert (-25)
|Prepare and cast a previously-unknown rite or spell

|Extreme (-50)
|Discern magical intent without extensive study

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Craft new rites or spells

|===
