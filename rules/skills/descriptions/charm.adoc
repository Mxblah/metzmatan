==== Charm

Ability to persuade others.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Convince a friend to do a small favor

|Simple (+50)
|Convince a neutral stranger to do a small favor

|Ordinary (+25)
|Perform with a proficient skill

|Straightforward (+10)
|Make a favorable and memorable impression

|Normal (0)
|Convince someone to do a moderate favor, improve standing with a typical person or group

|Hard (-10)
|Convince an unfriendly individual to do a small favor, improve standing with an unfriendly person or group

|Expert (-25)
|Convince a neutral stranger to do a large favor

|Extreme (-50)
|Convince a hostile individual to do a small favor, improve standing with a hostile person or group

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Convince a hostile individual to do a moderate or large favor

|===
