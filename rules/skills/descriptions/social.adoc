==== Social

Ability to integrate into and understand a group. Includes knowledge of rumors, social structures, and the like.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Learn of popular rumor

|Simple (+50)
|Recall broad knowledge of government and society

|Ordinary (+25)
|Learn of common rumor, obtain general knowledge of common groups

|Straightforward (+10)
|Fit in with a moderately different social group to your own, recall specific knowledge of government and society

|Normal (0)
|Learn of obscure rumor, obtain general knowledge of obscure groups

|Hard (-10)
|Fit in with significantly different social group to your own, learn of common secrets

|Expert (-25)
|Obtain specific knowledge of common or obscure groups, obtain general knowledge of secret groups

|Extreme (-50)
|Learn of well-protected secrets, obtain specific knowledge of secret groups

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Learn critical secrets, gain access to secret groups

|===
