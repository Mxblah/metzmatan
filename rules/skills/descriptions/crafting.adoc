==== Crafting

Ability to create and repair things. Art, furniture, armor, or more.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Perform basic maintenance or repairs on typical mundane objects

|Simple (+50)
|Perform basic maintenance or repairs on complex or magical objects

|Ordinary (+25)
|Create small or simple objects or art

|Straightforward (+10)
|Decipher the workings of a small or simple object

|Normal (0)
|Create fine or complex objects or art

|Hard (-10)
|Decipher the workings of a complex object

|Expert (-25)
|Decipher the workings of a magical object, create a magical object or art

|Extreme (-50)
|Create masterwork objects or art

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Create masterwork magical artifacts

|===
