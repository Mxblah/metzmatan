==== Electronics

Knowledge of and ability to repair and make use of electronic devices, including computers.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Use or maintain a computer or other consumer electronic device

|Simple (+50)
|Perform assembly, maintenance, or repairs on typical electronic devices

|Ordinary (+25)
|Operate or maintain industrial or nonstandard electronic devices

|Straightforward (+10)
|Write computer programs, determine functionality of simple devices or programs

|Normal (0)
|Override or hack simple electronic devices with appropriate tools

|Hard (-10)
|Decipher or repair complex devices or programs

|Expert (-25)
|Override or hack complex electronic devices

|Extreme (-50)
|Decipher magical electronic devices

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Override or hack magical electronic devices

|===
