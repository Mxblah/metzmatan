==== Perception

Ability to notice details, process sensory information quickly, and avoid surprise. Often used for initiative.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Identify an object in plain sight at a moderate distance

|Simple (+50)
|Identify an object in plain sight at a long distance or in dim light

|Ordinary (+25)
|Find simply hidden items, spot objects from afar or in dim light, initiative roll when ambushing

|Straightforward (+10)
|Identify or track individuals in a moderate crowd

|Normal (0)
|Find typical hidden items, typical initiative roll

|Hard (-10)
|Spot objects or creatures at night, spot individuals in a large crowd, detect pursuers

|Expert (-25)
|Spot small and well-hidden items, initiative roll when ambushed

|Extreme (-50)
|Disbelieve illusions, detect invisible items or creatures

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Spot magically concealed items

|===
