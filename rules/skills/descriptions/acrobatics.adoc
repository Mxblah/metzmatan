==== Acrobatics

Ability to move one's Body with speed and precision. Balancing, dodging, and the like. Secondary physical skill.

[cols="20,80"]
|===
|Modifier|Example task

|Trivial (+75 or more)
|Navigate uneven pavement

|Simple (+50)
|Avoid tripping on roots or other debris, somersaults

|Ordinary (+25)
|Balance on a board or beam, cartwheels

|Straightforward (+10)
|Squeeze into small opening

|Normal (0)
|Quickly walk or run across shifting sand or gravel, balance on smooth ice

|Hard (-10)
|Squeeze into opening narrower than shoulders, walk on a tightrope

|Expert (-25)
|Maneuver on a frictionless surface, squeeze into space barely fitting head

|Extreme (-50)
|Balance on fishing line

|Heroic (-75 or more)
|Leap between falling fragments in the air

|===
